## Images

| Image slot                   | Used on page types     | Recommended upload (px) | Max size | Crop rule                                                                      | Alt text    |
| ---------------------------- | ---------------------- | ----------------------- | -------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------ | ----------- |
| Masthead (Wide / Horizontal) | Story, Event, Training | 3840 × 1600             | 250 KB   | Scale to fill, crop around focal point.                                        | Informative |
| Thumbnail (Tall / Vertical)  | Story, Event, Training | 1600 × 1067             | 100 KB   | Used if set; otherwise crops the masthead image around the center focal point. | Informative |
| Person photo                 | About (team)           | 800 × 800               | 60 KB    | Center crop to square.                                                         | Name only   |

### General Recommedations

- Do not bake text, logos, or legends into photos: they get cropped and screen readers can't read them.
- Don't reuse one tight crop for both the masthead and cards. Upload a large original and set a focal point instead.
- Masthead images sit behind a fade and text: avoid very bright or busy images.
- Every image needs rights cleared under the NASA Image Use Policy.
- File format should be webp export.
